DYAD

A two-colour rhythm game in the browser. Notes come in amber or periwinkle: hit the matching colour on the beat, with either hand. Big notes take both hands.

Plays osu!taiko charts, with a built-in song list from easy to hard. Works on desktop with a keyboard and on phones with touch, and installs as an offline app.

Available in English, 한국어, 日本語 and 简体中文.
